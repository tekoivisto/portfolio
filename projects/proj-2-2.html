Throwing technique is of the utmost importance in the sport of disc golf. Players who are less physically strong but have better technique can typically throw further and more accurately than physically stronger players with poor technique. A commonly given technique advice is that the throwing arm should be like a whip: the arm is relaxed, and most of the power comes from the lower body. In this project, I simulated and optimized a mechanical disc golf launcher from scratch inspired by this technique advice.
